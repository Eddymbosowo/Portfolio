Professional Experience

Where I've Made an Impact

Lead Customer Identity & Access Management (CIAM) Consultant

Proactive Risk

April 2025 - Present

As a lead consultant, I managed a key project for a client in the online travel industry to optimize their Customer Identity and Access Management (CIAM) strategy. The work focused on reducing operational costs through a detailed analysis of over 2.4 million monthly active users, which identified opportunities to consolidate underused premium features. My role involved architecting a solution that established Okta as the central source of truth for user identities, using Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) and Apigee to synchronize data between modern and legacy systems in real-time. I also implemented a passwordless sign-in flow with Okta's Magic Links to significantly enhance the customer experience as part of the client's modernization and cost-saving roadmap.
